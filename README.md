## What I work on

- **Web & e-commerce:** building and maintaining the websites, shops and integrations behind my own brands.
- **Takeovers & succession:** taking over businesses and online projects whose owners are looking for a successor, and carrying them on instead of breaking them up.
- **Process automation:** improving repetitive workflows across content, purchasing, logistics and customer service.
- **Business operations:** turning ideas into sustainable day-to-day processes, from the first concept to ongoing operation.

Alongside my own businesses, I have worked in the technical infrastructure of Deutsche Telekom since 2010, today in project management for material and logistics.
